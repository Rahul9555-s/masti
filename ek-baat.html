Tumse Ek Baat Kehni Hai 😊
Main shayad tumhare saamne
apni feelings ko
utna express nahi kar pata.
Kabhi words nahi milte, kabhi sharm aa jaati hai,
aur kabhi darr lagta hai ki tum meri feelings ko
waise samjhogi bhi ya nahi. ❤️‍🩹
Lekin sach ye hai...
Tumhari presence
meri life mein bahut matter karti hai.
Tumse baat karne ke baad
chahe din kitna bhi difficult ho,
dil halka sa ho jaata hai. 🫶
Aur tumhari ek smile...
Pata nahi kyun,
mujhe andar tak khush kar deti hai. 😊
Shayad main tumhe har roz nahi bataunga...
Lekin tum mere liye
har roz special rahogi.
Aur haan, tumhari smile ki wajah banna
mujhe hamesha pasand rahega.✨
