Milestone 1: Day 1 & Aankhein 👀

Jab madam ka biometric lagwaya, tabse hi mujhe aapki aankhein sabse alag lagi. Main usi time se un aankhon mein gumm ho gaya tha. Lekin mujhe pata tha ki aapne Chairman section mein join kiye hai...or ministry ki jack se aaye ho... main baat karne se darta tha aapse.

Aapne shayad kabhi notice nahi kiya hoga, par main aapko Day 1 se dekhta aa raha hu. Aapko smile karta dekh ek alag hi sukoon sa milta tha. Door se hi dekh kar khush rehta tha aur sochta tha ki kya kabhi aapse baat hogi?

Milestone 2: Bahaane aur Smile 💻

Aise hi daily nikal jata tha. Fir jab aap Establishment mein shift hue, toh main jaan-bujh kar aata tha wahan, bas aapki smile dekhne. (Aur aapse request hai ab meri is nadaani par gussa mat ho jana!).

Kabhi Pawan ke PC ke bahaane, kabhi Dilip ke, toh kabhi Pushpa ke PC ke bahaane... main wahan zyada der tak rukta tha. Aur jab aap smile karte the toh bas dil garden garden ho jata tha! 😍

Milestone 3: The Number 📞

Maine Insta par bhi dhoonda, nahi mili. Fir bhagwan ne meri sun li! File transfer karne ke liye Dilip ne aapko mera number share kar diya. And I was like Balle Balle! 🕺

Maine jaan-bujh kar message kiya tha us time. Main toh bas wait kar raha tha ki aap kab mera number save karoge. Fir main status lagane laga ki kaash aap dekho. Shukar hai ye recruitment wala kaam shuru hua aur meri entry ho gayi.

Main aapse kuch bhi expect nahi karta, bas aapki ek smile ke ilawa. I know my limits, toh aap tension mat lo. Ab toh aapne mujhe thappad bhi maar diya hai (waise sach batau bohot tez ka maara tha aapne! 😂).

Main bas wo smile laana chahta hu, isliye ulti-seedhi harkatein karta hu. Baaki main pagal nahi hu... aapki smile mere liye bohot matter karti hai. Kabhi kisi situation mein phas jao, bas ek baar yaad kar lena mujhe.
